Frequently asked questions

Which is the best CRM for training institutes?

NNC CRM is purpose built for multi center training institutes and coaching centers. It captures every enquiry, manages admissions across all your branches, and gives you one clear view of performance, for one simple yearly price.

How do you promote a coaching center?

Capture enquiries from your website, ads, referrals and walk ins, then follow up fast on WhatsApp, SMS and email. NNC automates reminders and campaigns so your counsellors spend their time converting, not chasing.

Which is the best CRM for coaching centers?

A good coaching center CRM centralizes data across branches, tracks each student journey, and shows center by center analytics. NNC does all three and typically goes live in about a day.
